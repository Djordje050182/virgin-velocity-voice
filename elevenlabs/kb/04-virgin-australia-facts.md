## Corporate
- **ASX-listed June 2025**, ticker **VGN**, IPO price **A$2.90**, raised ~**A$685m**.
- Traded ~**21% below IPO** into 2026 (RE-VERIFY live price). Board/market pressure → lead with
  **disciplined, ROI-positive** tech spend, cost-to-serve and payback, not novelty.

## Financials
- **FY25:** group revenue ~**A$5.8bn**; pro-forma underlying NPAT ~**A$331m** (+27.8%);
  underlying airline EBIT ~**A$535m**; **20.7m passengers**; load factor **84.9%**; domestic OTP
  ~**76.8%**; transformation program delivered **>A$450m** gross benefits in FY25.
- **H1 FY26:** revenue ~**A$3.32bn**; underlying EBIT ~**A$490m**.

## Loyalty — the crown jewel
- **Velocity Frequent Flyer: 13m+ members** (official total; distinguish from "active"). The
  first-call brief said "8+ million" — 13m is Virgin's current official figure.
- ~**28% EBIT margin**, **80+ partners**, double-digit growth.

## Market position
- Domestic market leader ~**33% share** (led Qantas in Jan 2026). Duopoly with Qantas.
- Golden Triangle (SYD–MEL–BNE) revenue concentration. Capital-light: single 737 fleet +
  Qatar Airways wet-leased long-haul.

## Already on the AI path (the voice layer is the unclaimed wedge)
- Advertised for **conversational AI designers** (Sept 2025).
- **SabreMosaic** AI for ancillary offers; **Databricks** for data + AI.
- Signed an **OpenAI** deal (Nov 2025) for trip planning + an AI Trip Planner chatbot.
- **Voice** is the layer no incumbent has claimed — that is ElevenLabs' opening.
