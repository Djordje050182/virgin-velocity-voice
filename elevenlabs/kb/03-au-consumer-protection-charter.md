# Australian aviation consumer protection — regulatory context
> ILLUSTRATIVE FOR DEMO. Reflects publicly reported direction of the Aviation Consumer Protection
> reforms; treat specific figures as representative pending the final legislation.

## What's coming
- The **Aviation Consumer Protection Bill** was introduced to Parliament in **April 2026**.
- It establishes an **Aviation Consumer Ombuds Scheme** and a **Customer Rights Charter**.
- Key provisions reported:
  - **Automatic cash refunds** for cancellations (no need for the customer to chase).
  - **Tiered compensation for long delays** — representative band ~**A$150–A$600** for delays of
    roughly **3 hours or more**, depending on length.

## Why this matters for voice AI
- Carriers have warned the reforms could **"triple complaint volumes overnight"** and have
  forecast a need for roughly **40 additional customer-relations FTEs** just to process claims.
- A voice agent that proactively handles **cancellation, rebooking and refund-status** absorbs
  exactly this load: it informs customers of their automatic entitlements, initiates refunds or
  rebooking, and answers "where is my refund" — at scale, 24/7.
- This turns a regulatory cost centre into an automated, consistent, well-documented process.

## Agent guidance
- When a cancellation occurs, proactively tell eligible guests they are entitled to an automatic
  refund — don't make them ask.
- Be accurate and measured about compensation; if unsure of a specific figure, state the process
  and offer to confirm in writing, rather than guessing.
