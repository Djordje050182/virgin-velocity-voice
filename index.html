The use case · our differentiator

Everyone will demo the angry inbound call. We go the other way.

Proactive. Outbound. Multi-channel. Member-aware. The airline reaches out first.

    Ops event fires. Virgin’s disruption system flags flight VA838 — first delayed, then cancelled. simulated webhook

    Call 1 — delay. Guest Care proactively calls the guest: new departure, reassurance, “I’ll check back in 30.”

    Channel hop. Rebooking options arrive as a message thread. Guest picks one. simulated UI

Why ElevenLabs leads

Voice is your most emotional channel. Don’t water it down with a stack voice.

vs Hyperscalers

Google CCAI, Amazon Connect+Lex, Azure: cheap to bundle, generic voices. ElevenLabs is the premium voice layer — and you can run Gemini as the LLM inside it.

vs Agentforce Voice

Strongest counter — deep CRM actions, now open via MCP. Concede it honestly. But its native voice is generic; ElevenLabs can be the voice inside an Agentforce/MCP architecture. The axis shifts to voice quality + latency + model-agnostic.

vs CX specialists

PolyAI, Cresta, Sierra, Decagon. PolyAI is strong on containment but higher latency (700–900 ms) and managed-service. ElevenLabs: ~75 ms, self-serve speed, best raw naturalness.

The proof

Top TTS blind-test Elo · ~75 ms Flash · voice cloning for a branded Virgin voice · SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, EU residency, zero-retention · live CX: Revolut (8× faster resolution, 4M+), Klarna (35M, first-line phone), Deutsche Telekom (~80% resolution).

Stress-tested honestly: voice quality alone doesn’t resolve issues — containment depends on journey design, KB quality and graceful human handoff. ElevenLabs is the voice + orchestration layer, not a silver bullet.
