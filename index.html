    09
    The bigger idea For the Head of AI

    We don't replace your systems. We become the voice that drives them.

    ElevenLabs is the conversational intelligence layer. It listens, reasons fast, then reads from your data and hands work to the systems that own it. Including Agentforce.

    Reads, through Databricks

    Live booking and member context from your lakehouse, harmonising Sabre, Amadeus and Velocity. In this demo it's real — ElevenLabs runs a live SQL join through a secure proxy (MCP-ready).

    Acts, through Agentforce

    When a workflow belongs to Service Cloud — rebook, raise a case, push a refund — ElevenLabs calls your Agentforce agent over MCP, waits, and speaks the result back. Voice inside Agentforce, not versus it.

    This reframes the Salesforce question: you may well run Agentforce for CRM workflows. We're the voice layer in front of it — and in front of every other system. Honest note: in this demo the Databricks read and the Salesforce Case are real, over a secure proxy; the data is synthetic. In production these are live MCP / tool calls onto your systems.

    How we built this For the Head of AI

    One conversation. ElevenLabs orchestrates every system — live.

    This isn't a mock-up. ElevenLabs is the conductor: it listens, reasons on a fast model, then reads live from Databricks and acts live in Salesforce, then speaks the result back — all inside a single voice turn. The systems stay where they are; the voice drives them.

    What ElevenLabs orchestrates, turn by turn

    1. 1–2 · a disruption fires the call; the guest speaks — transcribed in real time.
    2. 3 · it reasons on a fast model and decides the next best action.
    3. 4 · reads live — a real SQL join in Databricks of Velocity loyalty ⋈ Sabre & Amadeus.
    4. 5–6 · acts live — creates a real Case in Salesforce Service Cloud / Agentforce.
    5. 7 · it composes the answer and Hannah speaks it — one seamless conversation.

    What's actually live here

    Real: the ElevenLabs voice agent; the Databricks lookup (live SQL join); the Salesforce Case (created via OAuth client-credentials). Synthetic: the member/booking data and the ops trigger.

    A secure proxy holds every credential, so the voice agent never touches a secret, and each call is parameterised and injection-safe. In production these become MCP / API calls onto your real Velocity, Sabre, Amadeus and Salesforce.

    The point: ElevenLabs isn't another point tool — it's the orchestration layer that conducts your existing stack. Databricks read + Salesforce act are live; data is synthetic
