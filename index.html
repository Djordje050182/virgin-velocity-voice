    07
    How it works

    One warm conversation, a disciplined graph underneath.

    Built on ElevenLabs' multi-agent workflow: subagent nodes, a deterministic tool node, and routing between them. Hover any node.

    Two things worth saying to your team. First, we model the Velocity lookup as a subagent here to show the orchestration. In production it's a tool call into Velocity. Second, the conversation runs on a fast model, but the entitlement decision is a deterministic tool node, so the model never decides who gets a free hotel.
